## 2026-08-06 — Founding day

### ECO-1 / RND-8 — Precedents (filed)
- **Canada penny phase-out (2012–13):** cash rounding to 5¢; Richmond Fed 2025: a U.S. phase-out could cost consumers ~$55.58M/yr — the rounding tax is regressive, paid by cash users. Killing a coin doesn't kill its cost; it moves it onto the poor.
- **India demonetization (2016):** 86% of currency invalidated overnight; ~99% returned; "no relatively healthy economy has ever carried out demonetisation" (BBC). Money out of style *by force* is trauma; *naturally* is opportunity. The difference is consent.
- **Weimar (1921–23):** anchor lost = faith lost. A currency needs a unit you can hold.
- **Historic denominations:** US half-cent (1857), UK halfpenny (1984), AU 1¢/2¢ (1991), Zim dollar (2009), Euro (2002 — agreement, not coercion, is how money changes identity).
- **Secondary use:** NYC subway token (1953–2003) became collectibles after retirement; penny smashing/jewelry legal under 31 CFR 82.2(b); wheat pennies; lucky pennies. A retired coin becomes a memory object — and memory objects carry value.
- **Tokens & scrip:** company scrip, wooden nickels, casino chips, local currencies (Brixton Pound, Ithaca Hours) — closed-loop currencies are lawful and workable.
- **Trading cards:** Pokémon +4,000% since 2004 vs S&P +480%; $12B market; Magic, baseball. Whimsy carries value; collection is representation.
- **Synthesis:** citizenship is a collection; each citizen is a card; the penny is the base card. The space is a trading-card economy where the cards are citizens.

### ECO-2 / RND-9 — Penny Comeback (filed)
- Campaign to bring the penny back: the cheapest anti-poverty policy nobody has proposed (removes the rounding tax).
- The penny anchors the cent; the cent needs a body.
- Fixed supply: 114B, last minted Nov 12 2025, Common Cents Act H.R. 3074 (passed House July 2026).
- **No-gouge principle:** stabilization, never speculation.
- Petition drafted, proper channels, published reasoning.
- All-branches-win matrix: penny returns / stays gone / partial — every branch wins because the real goal is intrinsic value.

### ECO-3 / RND-10 — Economic Doctrine (filed + simulated)
- The desire to feel special is historical value, not a flaw. Accept it; let it run until it's boring.
- The feedback loop: status markers die of success (positional goods). Embrace with technology → abundance → superiority stops being effective.
- **Robotic Class Doctrine:** robots end the servant class; no human is below another human; psychological class disparity loses its foundation.
- **Transition Doctrine:** no confiscation, no shaming, no breaking engines; capitalists → creators, workers → creators, everyone comfortable.
- Proper financing: penny anchor, WuBu Penny experiment, transparent economics, stability over extraction.
- **Simulation shipped and verified** (`tools/status_loop.py`): scarcity → status 1.00 forever (class locked); abundance → 0.80 (fades); feedback → 0.00 (DEAD — superiority is boring). The doctrine is now math.

### ECO-4 / RND-11 — The Land Question (filed + tools)
- **Georgism filed:** Henry George, *Progress and Poverty* (1879) — land is fixed, land value is social, rent is unearned; the remedy is a Land Value Tax on unimproved value. Champions: Smith, Ricardo, Friedman ("the least bad tax"), Stiglitz, Simon, Vickrey, World Bank, Australian Treasury.
- **The billboard:** the citizen's memory filed — the vacant-lot sign *"Everybody works but the vacant lot. I paid $35,000 for this lot and will hold it till I get $60,000"* (late 19th c. poster) and Fay Lewis's 1914 Rockford, IL billboard — economics education by public spectacle, first of its kind.
- **Boomers filed without rage:** captured beneficiaries, not cause — cheap housing (2.5× income then vs 7.1× now), rising wages, public investment, demographics; also their own cage (trickle-down lie, illiquid house-wealth, fear). Blame the structure, never the person.
- **Rage-free pedagogy (4 rules):** blame structure not person; file the position first; find the shared enemy (unearned capture); offer the transition not the accusation.
- **Boomer bridge:** acknowledge achievement, neutral math, grandfather past gains, shared target.
- **Understanding program:** economy as a filing system, billboards of the first ones (reviving 1914), the penny curriculum, the complaint file (rage = WBI-1 data).
