## What the Economics Division is

WuBuEconomics is the **economics division** of WuBuGov. Its mandate:

1. **Study every economic issue in the world.** Inflation, debt,
   disparity, housing, work, automation, money itself. No problem
   is too big to file; no problem is too small to understand.
2. **Create the solutions.** Not abstract theory — filed, tested,
   working solutions. The division's rule: *an economics that cannot
   be filed is not economics; it is vibes.*
3. **Bring back the penny.** The division runs the campaign to
   restore the penny — the unit that anchors the whole space — and
   to restore *intrinsic value* to money itself.
4. **Build the transition.** From a world of class and disparity to
   a world of comfort — a transition that capitalists can take
   without fear and poor people can take without shame.

## The three laws of WuBu economics

1. **The economy serves the first ones, not the other way around.**
   Every mechanism is filed against one question: does this let
   people live comfortably? If not, it is a cage wearing a suit.
2. **Money is a filing system for trust.** A currency that cannot be
   weighed, chained, and proven is a rumor. The penny is the most
   provable money in history — that is why it returns.
3. **Class ends when superiority becomes boring.** The division's
   most important idea (see `docs/economic-doctrine.md`): people
   want to feel special — and the *solution* is not to suppress
   that, but to let technology amplify it until everyone can feel
   special, and being special stops meaning anything.
